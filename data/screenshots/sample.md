# Community Garden Plan

Our plan for turning the empty lot on **Maple Street** into a garden the whole
neighborhood can share. Notes from every meeting are on the
[project page](https://example.org/garden), and anyone is welcome to help.

## Watering

The drip timer by the shed runs every morning:

```
zone 1   06:00   15 min   beds 1 to 4
zone 2   06:20   15 min   beds 5 to 8
```

## Build day

1. Clear the lot and mark out the paths
2. Assemble the beds and fill them with soil
3. Plant the first seedlings: *tomatoes*, *beans* and *herbs*

> Start small, keep it watered, and invite your neighbors.
> Everything else will grow from there.
